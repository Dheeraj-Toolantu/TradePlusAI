Synthetic fixtures for trader-scanner in India (NSE) mode. Nothing here is real market data: there is
no network in the build environment, so every file was generated by hand in the shape of the real
endpoint, with made-up prices and volumes.

EQUITY_L.csv                 NSE's equity list (archives.nseindia.com/content/equities/EQUITY_L.csv):
                             header with leading spaces after the commas, CRLF line ends, EQ / BE / BZ
                             series. 20MICRONS (BE) and SMALLBZ (BZ) must be dropped. Synthetic rows.
ind_nifty50list.csv          an index list (archives.nseindia.com/content/indices/ind_nifty50list.csv):
                             Company Name,Industry,Symbol,Series,ISIN Code. Seven synthetic members, one
                             of them (20MICRONS) not in the EQ series of EQUITY_L.csv.
wall5m-synthetic-2026-10-29.json
                             a bundle {"SYMBOL": <Yahoo v8 chart>} of 5-minute bars for Thu 29 Oct 2026,
                             9:15 to 15:25 IST (03:45Z to 09:55Z), meta currency INR, exchangeName NSI,
                             exchangeTimezoneName Asia/Kolkata, gmtoffset 19800.
                               RELIANCE   range high 1400.00, the 9:40 bar closes 1405.50 (hit at 9:45 AM)
                               M&M        range high 3200.00, the 9:30 bar closes 3212.40 (hit at 9:35 AM)
                               BAJAJ-AUTO drifts down, no breakout
                               TCS        the 9:35 bar is missing (null): unusable
                               PENNYCO    breaks out at Rs 31.50: under the Rs 50 price floor
                               THINCO     breaks out at Rs 203 on 50 shares a bar: under Rs 1 crore traded
daily-spark-synthetic-2026-10-28.json
                             a Yahoo v7 spark answer (closes only) of 40 NSE daily bars to Wed 28 Oct 2026,
                             stamped 03:45Z (9:15 IST), symbols with .NS.
HDFCBANK-1d-synthetic.json   a Yahoo v8 daily chart with volumes (6,000,000 a day for 20 sessions, then
                             4,000,000), for the 20-day average traded value.
